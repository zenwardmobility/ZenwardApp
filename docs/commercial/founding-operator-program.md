# Zenward — Founding Operator Program (Internal Draft)

**Status:** internal planning document only — not a public offer, not a contract, and no part of this program is hard-coded into the application. Pricing here is a starting proposal for internal review, not a committed rate; treat every number as provisional until Sales/Founder leadership signs off. Nothing in this document should be quoted verbatim to a prospect as a final offer without that sign-off.

## Purpose

Zenward's first real operators shape the product more than any spec document can. The founding operator program formalizes that relationship: a small, deliberately limited cohort of NEMT operators run a real pilot, in exchange for founding pricing and a direct channel to the product team.

## Structure

- **Cohort size:** deliberately small — target 3-5 founding operators for the first cohort, not an open rolling signup. Small enough that every operator's feedback is individually heard and actioned.
- **Pilot length:** 90 days from go-live (not from signup — go-live is when the operator's real trips start flowing through Zenward).
- **Cadence:** a structured weekly feedback conversation for the first 4 weeks, then biweekly through the remainder of the 90 days. Not ad hoc — scheduled, and someone owns showing up prepared with what changed since last time.
- **What the operator commits to:** real operational usage (not a sandbox trial), a member of their team available for the weekly/biweekly check-ins, and candid feedback — including negative feedback — as the primary value they provide beyond the pilot fee.
- **What Zenward commits to:** responsive support during the pilot window, transparency about what's built vs. not yet built (per `product-capability-matrix.md`), and a real say in near-term prioritization — not a promise that every request gets built, but a promise every request gets heard and responded to.

## Pricing (provisional — requires leadership sign-off before use in any external conversation)

- Founding pricing is discounted relative to eventual standard pricing — the exact founding rate is a business decision outside this document's scope; do not invent a number here or in a live conversation.
- No pricing of any kind is hard-coded into the Zenward application itself — pricing lives entirely in the sales process (proposals, contracts), never as an in-product plan/paywall structure, since none exists yet.

## Baseline and outcome measurement

To make the pilot's value legible (to the operator and to Zenward), define at pilot start:

- **Baseline metrics**, captured before or in the first week of go-live: trip volume/day, average time from "trip needed" to "trip assigned," how the operator currently discovers problems on a trip (and how long that takes), and any existing double-booking/no-show rate they can estimate.
- **Outcome metrics**, revisited at 30/60/90 days: the same measures, post-Zenward, plus qualitative feedback from dispatch and drivers.
- **Conversion decision point:** at the end of the 90 days, a defined conversation — not an automatic renewal or automatic churn — where the operator and Zenward jointly decide: convert to standard pricing, extend the pilot, or part ways. Document the decision and the reasoning either way.

## What this program is not

- Not a free trial with no structure — the weekly/biweekly cadence and baseline/outcome measurement are the point, not an afterthought.
- Not a discount available to everyone — the small cohort size and hands-on relationship are deliberate; this does not scale to every prospect that asks.
- Not a substitute for `sales-claims-boundary.md` — a founding operator still gets the same honest capability picture as any other prospect, not a rosier one because they're early.
