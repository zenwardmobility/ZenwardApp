# Zenward — Operator Discovery Questions

**Purpose:** questions to ask a prospective operator before or at the start of a demo, so the demo can be steered toward what actually matters to them rather than a fixed feature tour. Use 4–6 of these, not all of them — this is a conversation, not a form.

## Understanding their current operation

1. How many trips do you run in a typical day? How many drivers and vehicles?
2. Walk me through how a trip gets from "a facility calls" to "a driver is on the way" today.
3. What do you currently use to track drivers and assignments — paper, a spreadsheet, a whiteboard, another software product?
4. Do you get trip volume from a Medicaid broker, direct facility referrals, or both?

## Finding the real pain

5. When was the last time something went wrong on a trip — a delay, a no-show, a wrong pickup — and how did you find out?
6. How do you currently know whether a driver has actually left for a pickup, versus just being scheduled to?
7. If a family member called asking "what happened with my mother's ride yesterday," how would you find the answer?
8. What's the most time-consuming part of a dispatcher's day right now?
9. Have you ever had two people accidentally assign the same trip to two different drivers, or double-book a driver?

## Understanding constraints that shape the demo

10. Do your drivers use company phones, personal phones, or both?
11. Are there parts of your service area with unreliable cell signal?
12. Do you need a broker integration on day one, or is direct trip entry workable for a pilot?
13. Who else on your team would be using this day to day — just dispatch, or drivers too?

## Qualifying for the founding operator program

14. Would you be open to a structured pilot — a defined period, regular feedback conversations, in exchange for early pricing and direct influence over what gets built next?
15. What would "this pilot worked" look like to you, concretely, at the end of it?

## After discovery, before the demo

Use the answers above to pick which parts of the 10/15-minute demo script to emphasize — e.g., an operator whose biggest complaint is "I never know if a driver actually left" should get extra time on Steps 3–4 (driver lifecycle + location freshness); an operator worried about double-booking should get extra time on Step 2 (assignment safety).
